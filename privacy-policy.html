ABC Fun Land privacy policy

For the ABC Fun Land app (com.ansariatif.bmsutility.abcfunland) by Ansari Mohd Atif. Last updated 28 October 2026.

About this app

This privacy policy applies to ABC Fun Land, a learning game for young children available on Google Play (package name com.ansariatif.bmsutility.abcfunland). ABC Fun Land is developed and published by Ansari Mohd Atif, an individual developer (“we”, “us”).

The short version

  • ABC Fun Land has no login, sign-up or account.
  • We do not collect, store or share personal information about players.
  • The app asks for no device permissions such as camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.
  • The app shows child-appropriate ads from Google AdMob, configured for children.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. ABC Fun Land does not ask for a name, email address, phone number, photos, contacts or location, and there is no account to create.

Game settings (music and sound on or off, child lock) and progress are saved only on the device and are never sent to us. Letter and word voices are spoken by the device's built-in text-to-speech engine.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

ABC Fun Land is free and is supported by ads from Google AdMob, operated by Google LLC. Every ad request is marked as directed to children, and ads are limited to the “G” (general audiences) content rating, so Google serves only non-personalised ads and does not use the information for interest-based advertising or remarketing. The app does not request the Android advertising ID.

To show ads, prevent fraud and measure how ads perform, Google may automatically collect limited technical information from the device, such as:

  • IP address (used to estimate a general location, such as the country)
  • Device model, operating system and language
  • App-instance or diagnostic information about the ads shown or tapped

This information goes to Google, not to us, and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy and its policy for child-directed apps. AdMob is a Google Play Families self-certified ads SDK. We do not receive anything that identifies a player.

Children's privacy

ABC Fun Land is made for children and follows the Google Play Families policy and the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). It works without personal information: there is no account, no sign-up and nothing a child has to enter about themselves. Settings and outside links are behind a grown-ups-only question. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child's personal information has been shared with us, email codewithatif@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Sharing and selling data

Because we do not collect personal information, we have nothing to sell, rent or share. The only third party involved is Google AdMob, as described above.

Data retention and deletion

We keep no player data on our servers. Everything ABC Fun Land saves stays on the device, and uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it.

Security

Because no personal information leaves the device through us, there is no player data held by us that could be exposed. Ad traffic to Google is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Changes to this policy

If the app or this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy or ABC Fun Land? Email Ansari Mohd Atif at codewithatif@gmail.com.
