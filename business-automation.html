FAQ

Common questions

What kinds of tasks can be automated?

Anything repetitive and rule-based: data entry, report generation, moving data between tools, checking portals, sending reminders and testing websites.

What if the website or portal has no API?

I use Playwright to automate the browser the way a person would, with safeguards for slow pages and layout changes.

Where do the automations run?

On a small cloud server, your own server, or scheduled on existing infrastructure. I set up logging and alerts either way.
