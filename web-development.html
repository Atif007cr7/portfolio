Pricing

Pricing approach

Every project is quoted after a short discovery call, as a fixed price for a clear scope or an hourly/monthly estimate for ongoing work. The main things that affect cost:

  • Number of pages and unique page layouts
  • Custom design vs adapting an existing design
  • Admin panel or CMS requirements
  • Integrations (payments, CRM, WhatsApp, booking)
  • Content writing and migration from an old site

FAQ

Common questions

Can you build my website with an admin panel so I can edit it myself?

Yes. I can add a simple admin area, built with Laravel for example, where you can update text, images, services or products without touching code.

Will my website be SEO-friendly?

Yes. Every site gets a clean heading structure, unique titles and descriptions, fast loading, a sitemap, robots.txt and correct canonical URLs. Rankings also depend on content and competition, so I don't promise positions, but the technical foundation will be right.

Do you also host and maintain the website?

Yes. I can set up hosting on Hostinger, cPanel or AWS, connect your domain and SSL, and maintain the site afterwards with updates, backups and fixes.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. I rebuild it on a faster stack and keep your important URLs (or redirect them properly) so you don't lose existing search traffic.
