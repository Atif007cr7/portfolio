Pricing

Pricing approach

Every project is quoted after a short discovery call, as a fixed price for a clear scope or an hourly/monthly estimate for ongoing work. The main things that affect cost:

  • Number of apps, environments and databases
  • AWS vs shared/VPS hosting
  • Migration from an existing server
  • Monitoring and on-call expectations
  • Monthly maintenance vs one-time setup

FAQ

Common questions

Can you move my website or app to AWS?

Yes. I plan the migration, move the app and database, switch DNS with minimal downtime and keep the old server as a fallback until everything is verified.

Do you offer monthly server maintenance?

Yes. Updates, backups, monitoring, SSL renewals and fixes on a monthly basis.

Should I use AWS or regular hosting?

For a business website, good shared or VPS hosting is often enough and cheaper. AWS makes sense for APIs, apps with growing traffic and more control. I'll recommend based on your needs and budget.
