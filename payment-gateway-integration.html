FAQ

Common questions

Do you integrate Razorpay in Flutter apps?

Yes. I integrate Razorpay checkout in Flutter apps and verify every payment on the backend before confirming the order.

Why do I need webhooks if checkout already works?

Because users close apps, networks drop and callbacks fail. Webhooks let your server confirm the payment independently, which prevents unpaid orders and missing ones.

Can you fix my existing payment integration?

Yes. I'll audit the flow, fix verification and status handling, and add logging so issues are traceable.
