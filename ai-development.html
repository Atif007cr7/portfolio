FAQ

Common questions

Can you add AI to my existing app?

Yes. I usually add AI as a backend service (for example with FastAPI) that your current web or mobile app calls, so your app doesn't need a rewrite.

Will my data be private?

We choose the setup based on your data. Hosted APIs with appropriate settings, or local models running on your own server when data must not leave it.

How do you make sure the AI gives correct answers?

By grounding answers in your own documents (retrieval), adding guardrails, and testing on real questions from your business before launch. AI can still make mistakes, so we design the workflow with a human check where it matters.
