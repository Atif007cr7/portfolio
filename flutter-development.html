FAQ

Common questions

Why Flutter instead of separate native apps?

One codebase for Android and iOS means lower cost, faster releases and one set of bugs to fix. For most business apps the performance is indistinguishable from native.

Can you work with my existing Flutter code?

Yes. I'll review the code, flag risks and continue development, or refactor the parts that are slowing you down.

What is Serverpod?

Serverpod is a backend framework written in Dart. It lets the app and server share models and code, which is a good fit for Flutter-first teams. I also build backends with Laravel, FastAPI and Node.js.

Can I hire you part-time or for a fixed scope?

Both work. Fixed scope suits a defined app or feature. Ongoing monthly support suits continuous development.
