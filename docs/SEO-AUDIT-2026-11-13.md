## What already works (keep it)

- Content is server-rendered: 1,930 words visible without JS. AI crawlers that skip JS see everything.
- robots.txt names every AI search bot. GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, Claude-SearchBot and
  PerplexityBot all get 200 with the same bytes as a browser (no WAF challenge).
- `llms.txt` and `llms-full.txt` pass Lighthouse rules. Google ignores llms.txt, so it doesn't
  help rankings, but it does no harm.
- Apex → www 308, clean URLs, self-canonicals, real 404, Bing verification, IndexNow script.
- Service pages and the blog post carry FAQPage, BreadcrumbList, Service and BlogPosting JSON-LD
  with dates and author.

## Prioritised action plan

### CRITICAL: authority and off-page (the main reason the site isn't popular)

1. **Get the first 20–30 real referring domains.** Each of these is a legitimate link or mention:
   - GitHub profile README and every public repo's `homepage` field → ansariatif.tech
   - LinkedIn: Website field, Featured section, and a post per blog article
   - Cross-post each blog article to dev.to, Hashnode and Medium with `canonical_url` set to the
     original on ansariatif.tech
   - Freelance profiles that link out: Upwork, Fiverr, Contra, Clutch, GoodFirms, Peerlist,
     Read.cv/Polywork. The homepage already has Fiverr-style "I will…" gigs but no live gig links.
   - Ask clients (MyJobsMate, Yummy Tiffins, StartWell, etc.) for a "Built by" credit link and a
     short quote you can publish
   - Answer Flutter/Serverpod questions on Stack Overflow and r/FlutterDev, linking only when the
     post directly answers the question
2. **Create a Google Business Profile** as a service-area business (address hidden). It is the
   strongest free entity signal for "freelance developer in <city>" searches.
3. **Confirm Search Console and Bing Webmaster are verified and the sitemap is submitted.** No
   `google-site-verification` meta tag was found, so verification must be by DNS; check it.

### HIGH: entity clarity (helps Google knowledge panel and AI answers name you correctly)

4. Add a real headshot: `/assets/atif.jpg` (and WebP), shown on the homepage, and referenced as
   `Person.image` in JSON-LD.
5. Expand `Person.sameAs` to every profile from step 1 (GitHub, LinkedIn, dev.to, Upwork, Fiverr,
   Clutch, Stack Overflow, YouTube, X). Use the same name ("Ansari Mohd Atif") on all of them.
6. Add an `/about` page typed `ProfilePage` with `mainEntity` = the Person. Cover your city, years
   of experience, education and employer history, and the apps you built. AI engines quote this
   kind of first-party bio page.

### HIGH: proof (E-E-A-T) and images

7. Turn the 11 shipped apps into case-study pages (`/projects/<slug>`): problem, your role, stack,
   2–4 screenshots, store links, measurable result. These are the most citable, unique pages you can
   make, and they fix the "0 images" homepage.
8. Add a testimonials section with real, attributed client quotes. Do not add `Review`/`AggregateRating`

   - meta description 249 chars → ≤155
   - title 68 chars → ≤60

   - no JSON-LD (add WebPage + BreadcrumbList)

### MEDIUM: content and AEO

11. Expand `/blog` (129 words, flagged as thin) with an intro and topic hubs once there are 3+ posts.
12. Add a "Pricing / how much does an app cost in India" answer block on the homepage: a direct

### MEDIUM: measurement

    Analytics or Plausible so you can see whether any of this works.

### LOW

15. Optional: `Content-Signal: search=yes, ai-input=yes, ai-train=no` in robots.txt. It states a

## Expectations

A 2-month-old domain with no links rarely ranks for competitive terms like "freelance developer
India" before 6–12 months. The quickest wins are branded searches ("Ansari Mohd Atif"),
long-tail technical posts (Serverpod, Flutter + Razorpay) and the GBP listing.
